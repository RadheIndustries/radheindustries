Services

Casting

Casting is a highly efficient manufacturing process used to produce complex metal components in both ferrous and non-ferrous materials. It allows the creation of intricate shapes with excellent dimensional accuracy, making it ideal for a wide range of industrial applications.

Radhe Industries specializes in delivering premium-quality cast components using advanced methods such as Investment Casting, Die Casting, and Sand Casting. We manufacture parts in compliance with international standards including DIN, JIS, and BS, ensuring reliability and performance.

Machining

Machining is a precision manufacturing process used to shape metal and other materials into highly accurate components through advanced cutting, drilling, and finishing operations. It is essential for producing parts that require tight tolerances, smooth finishes, and exact specifications.

Radhe Industries offers high-quality machining solutions using state-of-the-art equipment and skilled expertise. Our capabilities include CNC turning, milling, drilling, and finishing operations, ensuring precision-engineered parts that meet customer requirements.
